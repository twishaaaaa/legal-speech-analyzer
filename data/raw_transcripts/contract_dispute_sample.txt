The plaintiff entered into a written agreement with the defendant for the supply of machinery. The contract required payment within thirty days of delivery.
The defendant failed to pay and the plaintiff alleges fraud, saying the defendant never intended to honour the agreement. Counsel for the plaintiff asks the court to award damages for the breach.
The defendant's attorney denies liability and argues that the witness gave testimony that was not credible evidence. The judge will decide the matter at trial.
If the court finds negligence, it may impose a fine, and the defendant may file an appeal against the verdict.
